How to Record for Skate Lab

To get highest accuracy biomechanical extraction, follow these camera rules:

  • 45° Angle: Always film from a front-diagonal or back-diagonal angle. Do not film straight-on to the side or front.
  • Use Slo-Mo (120fps+): Using the highest framerate will yield the best accuracy
  • Full Body in Frame: Ensure the skater's head and board never leave the camera view.
AI Limitation Warning: Monocular (single-lens) AI cannot see through objects. During 180s or Shuvits, the body eclipses itself, which causes standard models to hallucinate. Skate Lab utilizes a dynamic visibility threshold that automatically fades out failing limbs.
